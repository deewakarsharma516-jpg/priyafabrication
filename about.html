Who We Are

Fabrication done right, on-site and on-time

Priya Fabrication is a Faridabad-based engineering firm specialising in industrial boilers and heavy steel fabrication. We handle the complete scope — design, in-house manufacturing, insulation, piping, and turnkey erection — so plants get a single accountable partner for their boiler house.

Our range covers boilers and thermic-fluid heaters, industrial chimneys, wet scrubbers, ETP/STP tanks, IBR and non-IBR piping, conveyors, coal crushers, and the full catalogue of boiler-house spares and valves.
